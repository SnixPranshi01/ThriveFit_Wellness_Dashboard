## ThriveFit – Wellness in Every Dimension
ThriveFit is a modern wellness dashboard that helps users track and improve their overall well-being through a holistic approach to health management. By combining fitness, nutrition, sleep, mental wellness, and goal tracking in one intuitive platform, ThriveFit provides meaningful insights that empower users to build healthier habits and achieve long-term wellness goals.

The dashboard transforms everyday wellness data into actionable recommendations, making it easier to monitor progress, stay motivated, and maintain a balanced lifestyle. With interactive visualizations and a user-friendly interface, users can gain a complete view of their health journey and make informed decisions to improve their physical and mental well-being.

### Features
📊 Wellness analytics and insights
💪 Fitness and activity tracking
🥗 Nutrition monitoring
😴 Sleep and recovery analysis
🧠 Mental wellness tracking
🎯 Goal setting and progress monitoring
📈 Interactive dashboards and visualizations
📱 Responsive and accessible design
ThriveFit is built to help users thrive in every dimension of wellness by making health tracking simple, insightful, and engaging.
